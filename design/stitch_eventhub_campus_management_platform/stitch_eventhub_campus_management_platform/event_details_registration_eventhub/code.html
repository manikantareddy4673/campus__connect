About the Event

The Annual Intercollegiate Hackathon brings together over 400 passionate student developers, UX designers, machine learning researchers, and entrepreneurs across regional universities. Over 48 sleepless yet exhilarating hours, you will collaborate to engineer next-generation applications powered by modern generative AI, distributed systems, and real-time campus micro-utilities.

Whether you are building your first API integration or fine-tuning edge multi-modal models, our on-site mentors from regional tech firms and campus faculty will guide your team from initial ideation to the final demo stage. Hardware kits, cloud infrastructure credits, and round-the-clock catering are fully provided.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a pre-formed team before registering? expand_more

No! Many participants register solo. We host a structured team formation mixer on Day 1 where you will meet teammates based on mutual track interests and complementary skills.

What if I am a beginner with zero AI background? expand_more

The hackathon is beginner-friendly. We offer starter templates, non-technical tracks (UX/Product Strategy), and dedicated mentor stations to get you up and running within hours.

Is participation completely free for students? expand_more

Yes, 100% free. Thanks to campus department sponsorship and industry partners, all meals, energy drinks, API access credits, and event swag are covered.
